A Family Heritage House

A little piece of Melaka in the highlands of Sabah

Tranquera House was created by a Baba Nyonya family from Melaka who found a new home in Kundasang. Here, Peranakan heritage, heartfelt hospitality and the cool mountain air come together.

More than a place to sleep, Tranquera is a home where guests can slow down, enjoy traditional flavours and experience the warmth of a family-run stay.
